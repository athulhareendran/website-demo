Simple answer: the new night hours add about 15 trips a day (7% of all trips). Most of the growth, about 127 more trips a day, came in the normal hours because more cars joined and demand grew, so look at trips per car (4.0 → 8.8) to judge the 24-hour change fairly. Most night trips are at 2 AM (8 a day); 3–7 AM is quiet, about 1 trip an hour.
